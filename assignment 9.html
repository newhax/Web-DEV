2) Styles for links

Hover over a link, press and hold it, and click it to see each state.

Link States CSS Links (W3Schools) :visited (MDN) Assignment 8
SelectorWhen it appliesStyle used
a:linkLink not yet visitedBlue text, light blue background
a:visitedLink already visitedPurple text, light purple background
a:hoverMouse pointer is over the linkWhite text, orange background, underline
a:activeLink is being clickedWhite text, red background

Note: a:hover must come after a:link and a:visited, and a:active must come after a:hover (LVHA order).
