Welcome to the Online Book Store

This website lets students of all engineering departments browse and buy textbooks online. Books are arranged department-wise (CSE, ECE, EEE and CIVIL) so you can quickly find what you need.

  • Login – sign in to your account.
  • Registration – create a new account.
  • Catalogue – view all books with cover, author, publisher and price.
  • Cart – see the books you have added and the total amount.

Click a department on the left to view the books for that department.
